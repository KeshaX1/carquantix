VAELUNTHRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

App and developer identification

  • App: VAELUNTHRA
  • Package: com.kerim.vaelunthra
  • Developer: CarQuantix (Kerim Orazov)
  • Privacy contact: carquantix@gmail.com

This policy applies only to the VAELUNTHRA Android application and explains how data is handled when the app is used.

1. Data stored on your device

VAELUNTHRA stores game progress and preferences locally on the user's device. This includes resources, heroes, hero health, teams, buildings, construction timers, missions, tutorial progress, settings, and acceptance of the startup notice.

This local game data is not uploaded to CarQuantix servers. The app does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or payment information.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

VAELUNTHRA offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. An ad is requested when the user chooses the rewarded-ad option. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad views, clicks, completion status, and other ad interactions
  • Device information, app performance data, and diagnostics
  • Advertising consent choices where applicable

This information may be used to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising and, where permitted, to personalize ads.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

Locally stored information is used to save and restore the user's game state. Advertising-related information is processed by Google and its partners to deliver the requested ad, confirm completion, prevent abuse, measure performance, and maintain ad services.

4. Children

VAELUNTHRA does not knowingly ask children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information. Parents or guardians may contact the developer with privacy questions using the address below.

5. Data sharing and sale

CarQuantix does not sell personal information collected through VAELUNTHRA. Advertising-related data may be collected or processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above. CarQuantix does not receive the user's locally stored game progress or settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the user clears the app's storage or uninstalls VAELUNTHRA. Because the app has no user accounts and CarQuantix does not receive this local data, CarQuantix cannot remotely access or delete it.

Advertising data is retained and deleted according to the policies and controls of Google and its advertising partners. Users may reset or delete their advertising identifier through their Android device settings.

7. Data security

Local game data is protected by the security controls of the user's device and operating system. Data processed by Google services is transmitted and handled using Google's security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will remain available at this URL, with the revision date shown above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning VAELUNTHRA, contact the developer at carquantix@gmail.com.
