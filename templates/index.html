How to read the numbers

Use horsepower, acceleration, top speed, price, and consumption together. A faster car is not automatically the better ownership choice if it costs more to fuel, insure, maintain, or depreciates faster.

Data and methodology

CarQuantix comparisons are intended for research and shortlisting. Market, trim, tire, battery, and model-year differences can change the exact figures, so important purchase details should be confirmed before buying.
