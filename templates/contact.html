Contact

You can reach CarQuantix for support, partnerships, product questions, or vehicle data corrections.

  • Email: carquantix@gmail.com
  • Business and partnership email: carquantix@gmail.com
  • Website: CarQuantix platform

We usually respond within 1-2 business days.

What to include

For vehicle data corrections, include the model name, model year, market, trim, and the source you are using. For account or billing questions, include the email address connected to your CarQuantix account but do not send passwords or full payment card details.

Editorial and data requests

CarQuantix reviews feedback about missing vehicles, unclear comparisons, pricing assumptions, fuel or charging estimates, and usability issues. We cannot guarantee that every requested vehicle will be added immediately, but clear source information helps us prioritize updates.

Who can contact us

Drivers, buyers, automotive enthusiasts, journalists, data partners, and businesses can contact us about the platform. Messages about advertising, partnerships, corrections, and product support are handled through the same email address.
