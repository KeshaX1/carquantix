Privacy Policy

CarQuantix

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a) Information you provide

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account login details
  • Messages you send through contact forms

b) Automatically collected data

When you use CarQuantix, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address

  • Usage data (pages visited, features used)

This helps us improve our service and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services

  • Prevent fraud or abuse

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Payments

Payments are processed securely through Paddle. We do not store your credit card or payment information on our servers.

4. Cookies

CarQuantix may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences

  • Analyze usage trends

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

If Google AdSense or other Google advertising services are enabled, Google and its partners may place or read cookies in your browser and may use web beacons or IP addresses to serve, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to your settings and applicable law.

You can manage ad personalization in Google My Ad Center and learn more from Google's advertising technology information.

5. Data Sharing

We may share information with trusted third-party services only when necessary to operate the platform, such as:

  • Payment processors
  • Hosting providers
  • Analytics services
  • Advertising and measurement partners, when ads or analytics are active

These partners are required to protect your data.

CarQuantix uses Google Analytics to understand site usage and may use Google AdSense for advertising. Users can manage many Google ad personalization settings through their Google account and browser controls.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure.
